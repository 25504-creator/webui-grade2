Have questions? We've got answers. If you can't find what you're looking for, feel free to contact us.

What's the difference between the Free and Pro plans?

The Free plan gives you access to our introductory courses and basic community support. The Pro plan unlocks everything: all 200+ courses, guided Career Paths, professional certifications, exclusive projects, and priority support.

Can I cancel my Pro subscription at any time?

Yes, absolutely. You can cancel your monthly or yearly subscription at any time from your account settings. You will retain Pro access until the end of your current billing period.

Do I get a certificate upon completion?

Yes! Pro members receive a professional, shareable certificate for every Career Path and Skill Path they complete. You can add these to your LinkedIn profile and resume to showcase your skills.
