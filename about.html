About CodeKhmer Learning

We are dedicated to providing high-quality, accessible programming education for everyone. Our mission is to empower the next generation of developers in Cambodia and beyond by making learning to code intuitive, engaging, and practical.

Our Mission

To break down the barriers to learning technology. We provide hands-on, project-based courses that equip learners with the real-world skills needed to succeed in the tech industry, regardless of their background.

Our Vision

To be the leading platform for tech education in Southeast Asia, fostering a vibrant community of innovators, problem-solvers, and future leaders who will shape the digital landscape of tomorrow.
