Lesson 10: HTML Audio & Video

📖 Embedding Media

HTML5 introduced the <audio> and <video> elements, making it easy to embed media files directly into your webpages without needing plugins.

🎵 The <audio> Element

The <audio> tag is used to embed sound content in a document, such as music or other audio streams.

<audio controls>
  <source src="horse.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
  Your browser does not support the audio element.
</audio>

The `controls` attribute adds audio controls, like play, pause, and volume.

🎬 The <video> Element

The <video> tag is used to embed video content.

<video width="320" height="240" controls>
  <source src="movie.mp4" type="video/mp4">
  Your browser does not support the video tag.
</video>

Common attributes include `width`, `height`, `controls`, `autoplay`, `muted`, and `loop`.

💡 Tips

  • Always include the `controls` attribute for a better user experience.
  • Use the <source> tag to specify multiple media formats for better browser compatibility.
  • The `autoplay` attribute often requires the `muted` attribute to work in modern browsers.
  • For embedding videos from services like YouTube, use their provided `<iframe>` embed code.
